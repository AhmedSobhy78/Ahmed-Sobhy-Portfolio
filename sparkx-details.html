SPARK X RACING TEAM

Electrical Team Leader
Formula Student

As the Electrical Team Leader of Spark X Racing Team, I led the electrical systems division in designing, building, and testing the embedded systems powering our Formula Student race car.

Key areas of involvement:

Embedded Systems EV Technologies Battery Management High Voltage Systems Wiring Harness CAN Bus Sensor Integration Team Leadership

Battery & Power Systems

Designed and tested the battery pack and power distribution system for the Formula Student EV. Worked on battery cell selection, BMS integration, and high-voltage safety protocols.

Embedded Systems & Wiring

Developed the wiring harness and embedded control systems for the vehicle. Integrated sensors, actuators, and microcontrollers communicating over CAN bus for real-time data acquisition.

Testing & Validation

Conducted electrical testing and validation of all onboard systems. Diagnosed faults, optimized power consumption, and ensured reliability under racing conditions.
