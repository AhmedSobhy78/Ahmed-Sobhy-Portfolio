Electrical Team Leader at Spark X Racing Team — designing, building, and testing the embedded systems behind our Formula Student race car.

01

Overview & Role

The team, the project, and my responsibilities.

Overview

Spark X Racing Team is a student engineering team that designs, builds, and races a Formula Student car. As Electrical Team Leader, I lead the division responsible for the car's embedded systems — from power delivery to communication and safety.

My Role & Responsibilities

  • Designing and integrating a lithium battery pack with a BMS (Battery Management System) for safe and efficient power delivery.
  • Developing the wiring harness and CAN bus communication network connecting the VCU, BMS, motor controller, and sensors.
  • Implementing high-voltage safety protocols compliant with Formula Student regulations.
  • Conducting electrical testing, fault diagnosis, and performance optimization of all onboard systems.
  • Leading a team of electrical engineering students, coordinating tasks and managing project timelines.
