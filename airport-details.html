Cairo International Airport 1 Month Engineering Training

One-month engineering training at Cairo International Airport covering HVAC systems, chillers, diesel generators, and electrical and backup power systems supporting airport operations.

01

Overview & Role

The training and what it covered.

Overview

A one-month engineering training at Cairo International Airport, focused on the engineering systems and infrastructure that support airport operations.

What I Learned

  • Gained practical exposure to HVAC systems, chillers, diesel generators, and general electrical power systems supporting airport operations.
  • Learned how electrical, mechanical, and backup power systems are interconnected and coordinated to maintain continuous power and reliable airport operations.
  • Explored the airport's engineering infrastructure, including rooftop HVAC equipment, fire protection systems, and critical operational facilities.
  • Understood the importance of reliable infrastructure in airports and other critical facilities, where power interruptions or operational delays can significantly impact safety, efficiency, and travel operations.
