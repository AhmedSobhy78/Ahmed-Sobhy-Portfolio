01

Overview

What the project is and what it does.

This project is a cam design study focused on designing a cam mechanism and analyzing how the follower moves and how forces act through the mechanism.

It covers cam profile generation, follower motion analysis, and force analysis, providing a complete understanding of how cam-follower systems convert rotational motion into controlled follower output.
