KUKA SMART SYSTEMS

Industrial Robotics Intern

KUKA Smart Systems October 2025 Egypt

Attended the “KUKA KSS 8.x – Operator & Programming” workshop at Smart Systems, KUKA's official training partner in Egypt. Gained hands-on experience in robot operation, motion programming, and automation.

01

Overview & Role

The company, the internship, and my responsibilities.

Overview

KUKA is a global leader in industrial robots and automation. Smart Systems is KUKA's official training partner in Egypt.

The workshop taught how industrial robots are programmed, operated, and integrated into real production systems.

My Role & Responsibilities

  • Operated the KUKA Teach Pendant (jogging and safety modes).
  • Wrote and executed basic robot programs on KUKA KSS 8.x.
  • Practiced motion programming and path control.
  • Saw how robots integrate into production lines and smart manufacturing.
