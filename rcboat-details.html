RC BOAT

Embedded Wireless Remote-Controlled Boat

University Project University Event Embedded Systems

Designed and showcased an RC boat with an embedded control system and wireless remote control, presented to students, faculty, and university leadership.

01

Overview

What the project is and what it does.

This project is a remote-controlled boat with an embedded control system, powered by a microcontroller and driven by a motor driver with wireless radio control.

I designed and showcased the boat at a university event, presented it to prospective students, faculty members, and university leadership, and received a Certificate of Appreciation for promoting hands-on engineering and innovation.
